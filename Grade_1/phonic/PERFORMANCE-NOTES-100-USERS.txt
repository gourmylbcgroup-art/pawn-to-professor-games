Alphabet Adventure — 100-User Online Sound Optimization

WHAT I CHECKED

There are two different sound systems in this app:

1. Jia-Jia prerecorded lesson voice
   - Embedded directly inside app.js as one MP3 data bank.
   - It does NOT need an online request at playback time.
   - It is decoded once into a Web Audio AudioBuffer and reused.

2. Balloon Battle sound/music
   - Sound effects/pop are generated or embedded in memory.
   - Background music is the separate file:
     assets/audio/coin-op-groove.mp3

IMPORTANT: Chrome SpeechSynthesis is NOT used in this version.

ONLINE-LAG CHANGES

Jia-Jia voice:
- Decode is de-duplicated through one shared Promise.
- AudioContext is resumed on the first user interaction.
- Once decoded, clips play entirely from RAM.
- No per-letter / per-word network request is made.

Balloon Battle music:
- It no longer downloads on the Home screen.
- It loads only when Balloon Battle needs it.
- It is fetched once with browser force-cache.
- The downloaded MP3 is converted to a local Blob URL.
- Playback then uses the local Blob instead of repeatedly streaming/seeking online.
- The original MP3 file/quality is unchanged.

100-USER IMAGE OPTIMIZATION
- 57 large PNG images converted to high-quality WebP.
- Converted image bytes: 62.4 MB -> 4.4 MB.
- Images use asynchronous decoding.
- Non-home static images use browser lazy loading where applicable.

PACKAGE SIZE
Before: 64.6 MB unpacked
After:  6.7 MB unpacked

RESULT
The two network-sensitive areas have been separated:
- Jia-Jia voice plays from embedded/in-memory audio.
- Balloon music downloads only when needed and then plays from a local cached Blob.

This greatly reduces the chance of online audio lag for a large class.
No browser app can guarantee zero delay on every school Wi-Fi network, but normal Jia-Jia voice playback after the initial decode does not depend on the internet.
