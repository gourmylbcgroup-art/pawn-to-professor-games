WORLD FOOD CHICKEN FEATHER CHASE
1. Select 2–4 differently colored chicken tokens.
2. Six highlighted feather squares (+1 feather on landing).
3. First to reach 5 feathers wins; all players start with 0.
4. Correct match + speaking = move one space and continue same turn.
5. Wrong match = next player after 2.6 sec reveal.
6. Overtake by landing on another chicken: steal 1 feather if available.
7. On every restart, shuffle all 24 track spaces and all 12 mystery cards independently, plus the feather squares.
8. A revealed card returns face-down after the turn; card locations remain fixed until restart.
