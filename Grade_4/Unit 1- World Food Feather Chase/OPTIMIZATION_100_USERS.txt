World Food Feather Chase – 100 concurrent visitor optimization

Static client-side HTML5 game, no game server needed for local matches.
Card art converted from PNG to WebP (900x1125 maximum), background to WebP.
Shared audio player and beep AudioContext reduce browser allocations.
Preloads key board and card-back images; voice audio remains on demand.
All game rules, board layout, mystery card arrangement, and movement unchanged.

For production: host on CDN (Cloudflare Pages or equivalent); set long Cache-Control immutable for versioned /assets/ paths, use compression on HTML, and keep the site behind HTTPS.
100 simultaneously loaded users is an infrastructure target, not independently load-tested here.
