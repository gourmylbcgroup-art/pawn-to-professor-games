GRANDMA VS GRANDPA — TUG OF WAR V50 (USER VOICE)

Launch index.html (standalone) or launch from the Grade 4 Unit 4 lesson GAME menu.
Uses the two TTSMaker exports the user uploaded on October 25, 2026. The 45 answer sentences, two victory announcements and draw announcement use the same recorded voice. Speech plays after the students answer, while the lights and rope show who pulled. No browser voice synthesis in this game.

Voice is packed into one voice-bank.js (2 embedded compressed recordings) and decoded in the browser after Start. No separate audio fetch for every flashcard. Music: user-supplied wackywobblings(2).ogg and Safari-compatible AAC. A recorded five-second countdown starts the two-minute match. Match rules and the approved clickable art are preserved.

Accessibility: each button has an accessible text label. A local/offline browser is supported; a live 100-user performance guarantee requires real hosted testing.
