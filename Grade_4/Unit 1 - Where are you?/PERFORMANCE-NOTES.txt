Where Are You? Game — 100-User Optimization

Goal:
Reduce bandwidth spikes when many students open the game at the same time.

Changes:
- Large PNG/JPG assets converted to high-quality WebP where useful.
- WAV audio converted to compact MP3 where possible.
- Audio uses preload="none".
- Images use asynchronous decoding.
- Embedded iframes use lazy loading.
- Only clearly unreferenced source/original heavy files were removed.
- Game logic, rules, navigation and visual design were preserved.

Image conversion:
Before: 18.5 MB
After:  1.7 MB

Audio conversion:
Before: 0.4 MB
After:  0.1 MB

Total unpacked size:
Original:  20.7 MB
Optimized: 3.5 MB

Actual classroom performance still depends on school Wi-Fi, device speed and hosting/CDN behavior.
