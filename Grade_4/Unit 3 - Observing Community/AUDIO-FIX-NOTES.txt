ONLINE AUDIO FIX

This build removes the online-only audio bottleneck.

Main lesson speech:
- Spoken audio is embedded directly inside index.html.
- No speechSynthesis is used.
- No voice MP3 is fetched when a card is clicked.
- Web Audio uses latencyHint='interactive'.
- The audio buffer is decoded as the page loads and unlocked on the first pointer/touch event.

Mime Game music:
- Background music is compressed and embedded directly in game/index.html.
- No music file is fetched after the game opens.

Result:
- Online and offline use the same bundled audio path.
- The page no longer depends on network speed at the moment a sound button is pressed.
