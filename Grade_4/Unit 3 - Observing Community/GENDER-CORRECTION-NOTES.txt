GENDER CORRECTION

The visible flashcards were checked and the action/pronoun mapping was corrected to:

jumping = He
singing = She
running = He
drawing = She
dancing = She
writing = He
reading = He
playing = He
eating = He
drinking = He

The correction applies to the dynamic activities that use the ACTIONS data:
- He / She practice
- Look and Guess
- Listen and Guess
- Community Reporter
- Action + Place
- Practice Challenge

For Action + Place, when a corrected full sentence is not present as one recorded clip,
the app now plays two existing recorded clips in sequence:
"[He/She] is [action]." + "at the [place]"
This avoids falling back to browser TTS and keeps the user's recorded voice.
