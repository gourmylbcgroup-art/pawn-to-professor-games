Grade 6 Unit 1 — Secret Person Sound Fix

Problem fixed:
The Hear Model voice used a hard-coded example:
  "Is he/she from Asia? Yes..."
  "Is he/she from Europe? No..."

That could contradict the actual secret person (for example, Kofi is from Africa).

Fix:
- Hear Model now uses the CURRENT secret person's real continent for the YES example.
- It automatically chooses a different continent for the NO example.
- Tapping an individual person card now uses that person's real continent in the model question.

Voice quality:
UNCHANGED.
- No audio files were compressed or converted.
- No TTS rate was changed.
- No TTS pitch was changed.
- The same browser speech-synthesis voice is used.
- Only the words sent to the voice were corrected.

Examples:
Kofi:
  "Is he from Africa? Yes, he is."
  followed by a truthful No example.

Emma:
  "Is she from Europe? Yes, she is."
  followed by a truthful No example.
