START — Let's Explore!

Week 1 · world map · Asia, Europe, Africa, America

WEEK 1

Touch the four continent names

Teacher: “Where is it?” Students point, listen and repeat.
Touch a continent to hear its name.

👀 Look

Find Asia, Europe, Africa and America on the map.

👂 Listen

Hear each continent and repeat together.

🗣️ Say

Use: “It is in ___.” after the teacher shows a flag.

Teacher script

Present the world map. Ask “Where are you from?” and model an answer. Point to Asia, Europe, Africa and America. Students repeat each name. Keep the first round visual and low-pressure.

AsiaEuropeAfricaAmericaWhere is it?It is in ___.

PLAY — Flag Challenge

Digital version of the 4 Corners idea · identify the continent before the reveal

WEEK 1 GAME

Where is it?

Choose a continent.
Teacher script

Show the flag. Students choose a continent. After the answer, have the class say the complete sentence: “Japan is in Asia.” You can also ask students to stand in a classroom corner first, then check on the screen.

Where is it?It is in ___._____ is in _____.

PRACTICE — World Practice

10 countries + flags · then animals, continents and sentence building

Teacher script

Use picture and word support first. Let beginners identify the continent before asking for the full sentence. Build from one-word answers to “I’m from ___.” Stronger students answer without the sentence frame.

Where are you from?I’m from ___.AustraliaAntarctica

SPEAK — Secret Person

Week 3 · he/she · yes/no questions · guessing game

WEEK 3

Secret Person

Choose a person. Then the class asks continent questions.
Is he/she from Asia?
Ask 2–3 questions before revealing.

Teacher-supported

Visible frame: Is he/she from _____?
Answers: Yes, he/she is. / No, he/she isn’t.

Independent

Hide the sentence frame. Students ask several questions, then guess the person.

Is he from ___?Is she from ___?Yes, he/she is.No, he/she isn’t.He/She is from ___.
