Grade 6 Unit 1 — Around the World
100-User Classroom Optimization

Goal:
Reduce startup bandwidth and unnecessary loading when many students open the lesson at the same time.

Changes:
1. Large PNG/JPG assets converted to high-quality WebP where this meaningfully reduced size.
   Image data before conversion: 4.0 MB
   Image data after conversion:  0.4 MB

2. WAV audio converted to compact MP3 where possible.
   WAV before: 0.0 MB
   MP3 after:  0.0 MB

3. Audio uses preload="none" so sounds are fetched only when needed.

4. Images use asynchronous decoding.

5. Embedded local iframes use lazy loading / deferred loading where safe.

6. Only clearly unreferenced heavy source/original files were removed.
   Removed files: 0

7. Lesson content, countries, sentence patterns, navigation, activities and visual design were preserved.

Total unpacked size:
Original:  4.1 MB
Optimized: 0.5 MB

This package is better suited to a larger classroom / around 100 simultaneous users.
Actual performance still depends on school Wi-Fi, browser/device speed and hosting/CDN behavior.
