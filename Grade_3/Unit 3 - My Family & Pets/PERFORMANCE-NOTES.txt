My Family & Pets — custom voice / 100-user optimized build

AUDIO
- All 100 spoken lesson items use the newly supplied four TTSMaker recordings, in the supplied order.
- The recordings are trimmed and packed into ONE compact mono MP3 audio sprite.
- Online: the sprite is fetched once, decoded once, then every Hear action plays from RAM with no per-click network request.
- Offline/local folder: a same-file HTMLAudioElement fallback plays the exact same prerecorded sprite; no browser/system voice is substituted.
- Service-worker cache version v5 forces browsers to replace the previous voice after deployment.

100-USER CLASSROOM OPTIMIZATION
- One compact audio request instead of 100 separate audio files/requests.
- Existing lazy-loading of lesson images is preserved.
- Full lesson images load only when the relevant section opens.
- Presentation preloads only the next slide and respects Save-Data / slow connections.
- Static visuals can be reused from the service-worker cache after first successful load.

Actual simultaneous performance also depends on hosting/CDN capacity and the school Wi-Fi, but this build minimizes avoidable browser and network overhead.
