MONSTER MISSION
4 PLAYERS • EASY → HARD • SPEAK BEFORE YOU CLICK

Monster Mission

Review colors first, then feelings, then combine everything in Monster Detective and Listen & Build challenges.

How to Play

1

Color Only

Recognize the color, say the word, build the full sentence, match objects, then let a monster ask “What color am I?”

2

Feeling Only

Recognize feelings, answer “He is ___,” then move to the monster’s voice: “How do I feel?” → “I am ___.”

3

Mixed Mission

Combine color + feeling, solve Monster Detective, complete Listen & Build boxes, then finish with clue challenges.

Class rule: say the answer aloud first, then click. Correct speaking choice earns a point; completing the game action earns another point. All four players stay in the game.
