1

START — Monster Mission Warm-Up

5 minutes • short chunks • song + fast color retrieval

🎯 OBJECTIVEActivate all seven color words quickly.
💬 LANGUAGEWhat color is it? → It’s ___.
🧩 WEEKWeek 9 entry
Teacher script & scaffolding

Keep START brief. Students first retrieve a color word, then expand to “It’s ___.” Use the song for movement and energy, not as a long teaching stage.

2

LEARN — Colors → Feelings

10 minutes • one model at a time • model → repeat → mini-check

🎯 OBJECTIVEConnect colors to monsters and feelings.
💬 LANGUAGEWhat color is it? → It’s ___. • How does the monster feel? → The monster is ___.
🧩 WEEKWeek 9 core

Mini-check

Teacher script & scaffolding

For each color, show the color flashcard first, then immediately check the SAME color with a real object: yellow→sun, green→leaf, red→apple, black→cat, pink→heart, blue→raindrop, white→cloud. Then connect colors to feelings.

3

PLAY — Monster Match

8 minutes • one strong guided game • say the sentence before matching

🎯 OBJECTIVEUse the Week 9 color–feeling connections accurately.
💬 LANGUAGEWhat color is it? → It’s ___. • How does the monster feel? → He is ___.
🎮 ACTIONChoose monster → say → match feeling
Teacher script & scaffolding

Students choose a monster, say its color, then match the feeling. Ask the feeling question after each match. The activity ends when all six monsters are matched.

4

PRACTICE — Monster Detective

10 minutes • Level 1 → Level 2 → Level 3 • support gradually removed

🎯 OBJECTIVEMove from identifying language to asking detective questions.
💬 LANGUAGEIt’s ___. • He is ___. • Is it ___? → Yes, it is. / No, it isn’t.
🧩 WEEKWeek 10
Teacher script & scaffolding

Level 1 allows short identification. Level 2 requires the complete sentence. Level 3 becomes a true Monster Detective: students choose their own color questions until the monster is found.

5

SPEAK — Listen & Build Mission

7 minutes • listen → say → build • less visual support

🎯 OBJECTIVEProduce the Week 11 language with increasing independence.
💬 LANGUAGEIt’s yellow / blue / pink. • How does the monster feel? • Are you happy?
🧩 WEEKWeek 11
Teacher script & scaffolding

Model Box 1 with yellow. Give less help for blue and pink. After the three boxes, connect the colors to feelings. Use the grass clue and personal feeling question only after the core build is complete.

6

5 minutes • 8 calm questions • one question at a time • teacher marks after speaking

🎯 OBJECTIVECheck independent use of the unit language.
💬 LANGUAGEColor • feeling • yes/no • clue • personal answer
