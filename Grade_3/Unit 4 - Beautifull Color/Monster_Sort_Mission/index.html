How to Play

1Touch the CENTER-EDGE card.
2Say the English answer aloud.
3Touch ANY other platform with free space.
4The selected monster flies to that platform.

Monster Sort rule: Only the card on the edge nearest the center of the board may move. New cards always join the BACK. On the left shelves this is the RIGHTMOST card; on the right shelves it is the LEFTMOST card. The bottom Magic Platform uses its exposed card. After selecting it, you may move it to ANY other platform that has fewer than 3 cards.

Back-of-platform rule: Every moved monster joins the BACK of the destination. It never jumps in front of the existing cards. The card nearest the center remains the FRONT and is the only movable card.

Complete group: When 3 matching monsters fill a platform, they celebrate and fly away. Mixed groups may also hold 3 cards, but you must rearrange them later. Clear all six matching groups to win.

Classic Puzzle: no timer. Use UNDO or RESTART whenever needed.

Team Race: every team gets the same puzzle. The timer runs, but illegal moves do not move and there is no time penalty. Fastest completion time wins.

Level 1 — Color: Say the sentence: “It’s red.”
Level 2 — Feeling: Say the sentence: “The monster is calm.”
Level 3 — Color + Feeling: Say both together: “The red monster is angry.”
