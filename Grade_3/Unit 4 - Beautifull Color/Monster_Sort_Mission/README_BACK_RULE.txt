MONSTER SORT MISSION — BACK-OF-PLATFORM RULE

Movement rule:
1. Select the CENTER-EDGE card.
2. Say the English.
3. Choose any destination platform with free space.
4. The moved card ALWAYS joins the BACK of that platform.

Visual orientation:
- LEFT shelves: FRONT = rightmost card, BACK = far-left.
- RIGHT shelves: FRONT = leftmost card, BACK = far-right.
- MAGIC platform: FRONT = rightmost card, BACK = far-left.

The incoming card never becomes the new front card unless all cards in front of it are later moved away.
