MONSTER SORT MISSION — CENTRAL EDGE RULES

FINAL MOVEMENT RULE:
1. Only the card on the platform edge nearest the center of the board may be selected.
   - LEFT shelves: RIGHTMOST card
   - RIGHT shelves: LEFTMOST card
   - Bottom MAGIC PLATFORM: exposed card
2. Say the English answer.
3. Move that card to ANY OTHER platform that has free space.
4. Each platform holds a maximum of 3 cards.
5. When 3 matching monsters are together, they celebrate and fly away.
6. There is only ONE empty MAGIC PLATFORM at the bottom center.

Important:
- Destination color does NOT need to match.
- A platform with 1 or 2 cards can receive the selected card.
- Full platforms cannot receive another card.
