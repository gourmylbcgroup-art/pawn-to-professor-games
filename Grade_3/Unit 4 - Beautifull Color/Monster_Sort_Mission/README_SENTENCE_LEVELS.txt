MONSTER SORT MISSION — SENTENCE PRODUCTION UPDATE

LEVEL 1 — COLOR SORT
Students say a complete color sentence:
“It’s red.”
“It’s blue.”
“It’s green.”

LEVEL 2 — FEELING SORT
Students say:
“The monster is calm.”
“The monster is sad.”
“The monster is angry.”

LEVEL 3 — COLOR + FEELING
Students combine both:
“The red monster is angry.”
“The blue monster is sad.”
“The green monster is calm.”

The sorting mechanics are unchanged:
- center-edge source card only
- destination may be any platform with space
- incoming card goes to the BACK
- one bottom Magic Platform
- groups of 3 matching cards clear
- Undo / Restart / music / team modes remain
