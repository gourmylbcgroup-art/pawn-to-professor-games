Numbers 1–10 — 100-User Classroom Optimization

Goal:
Reduce bandwidth and startup work when many students open the interactive lesson at the same time.

Changes:
- Converted the two largest PNG assets to high-quality WebP.
- Main lesson media already used compact WebP + MP3, so those were preserved.
- Audio preload changed from metadata to none where applicable.
- Images use asynchronous decoding.
- Home/menu image remains high-priority for a fast first screen.
- Existing games remain separate links, so they do not download until a student opens a game.
- Lesson content, voices, navigation, sentence patterns and game logic were preserved.

Unpacked size:
Original: 7.2 MB
Optimized: 3.4 MB
