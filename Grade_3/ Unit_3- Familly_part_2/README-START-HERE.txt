GRADE 3 — UNIT 3: FAMILY ADVENTURE
Curriculum update: Learn → Practice → Speak (3 levels)

START: Extract the entire ZIP and open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
Select Level 1, 2 or 3 from the coloured buttons at the bottom of HOME.
Select any of the 7 room signs. Use MAP (top-left) to return HOME.

LEVEL 1 — father, mother, brother, sister.
Question: Who is he/she? Answer: He/She is my ____.
LEVEL 2 — review Level 1, add grandfather, grandmother, uncle, aunt.
Question: Who is he/she? Answer: He/She is my ____.
LEVEL 3 — review all eight family members; add dog, cat, bird, fish, rabbit.
Teach THIS/THAT (near/far); introduce This is my father;
What is this/that? This/That is my ____.

LEARN: Fully supported cards with the vocabulary label, question, answer
and HEAR. FAMILY first. HE/SHE concept cards use family pairs;
THIS/THAT is available in Level 3. Sentence cards place the model text
on the left and picture on the right.
PRACTICE: Picture only, question and answer choices. A wrong answer
invites another try. Correct answer feedback appears after selection.
SPEAK: Model answer starts hidden; reveal enables its HEAR button.
PLAY: The speaking prompt matches the selected level.
PRESENTATION: The original 34 slides are preserved.
Level 1 displays 9 eligible slides. Level 2 displays 13.
Level 3 displays all 34, including the later THIS grammar.
The original house/backgrounds and flashcard artwork are preserved.

AUDIO: Existing prerecorded English voice segments are retained.
Where exact new phrases were not recorded, English browser text-to-speech
is used; actual available voices depend on the classroom browser/OS.
Use Chrome/Edge and check audio on the classroom machine.
ONLINE: If updating an existing deployment, overwrite the whole folder.
A new service-worker cache version is included to avoid stale pages.

QA: See AUDIT-CURRICULUM-V2.json for the automated browser checks.
Manual classroom microphone/speaker checks and production 100-user load
testing have not been performed.
