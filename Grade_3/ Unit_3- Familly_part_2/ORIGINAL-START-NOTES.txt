START FLOW
1) Full family welcome photo; tap Father, Mother, Brother or Sister. A speech bubble displays each welcome. Tap again or HEAR AGAIN to repeat.
2) MEET MOTHER advances to eight picture-and-speech introduction screens: Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Grandfather, Grandmother, Uncle, Aunt.
3) PREVIOUS returns one member, or to family photo from Mother. NEXT advances; after Aunt, GO TO LEARN opens LEARN.
4) Tapping the big family-member card speaks the complete greeting.

AUDIO NOTE
The original 100 prerecorded lesson audio entries are unchanged. New greeting sentences were not included in the source recording, so START uses the browser's English speech-synthesis voice (language en-US). It is dependent on installed voices/browser support, especially when opening files offline. To use your exact teacher voice, record the eight greeting lines and provide audio clips for integration.
