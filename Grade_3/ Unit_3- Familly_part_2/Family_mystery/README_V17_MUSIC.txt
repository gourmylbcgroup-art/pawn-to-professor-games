FAMILY MYSTERY HOUSE BATTLE V17 — BACKGROUND MUSIC

Music: user-provided wackywobblings.ogg (46.36 s)
Music begins when START is pressed, loops throughout gameplay, and pauses when the
teacher pauses the game, leaves the tab, or opens a confirmation/instructions overlay.
It resumes with the game and stops/reset when the game finishes or returns Home.
The small note (♫) beside SCENERY turns music on or off independently.

Music is quieter than the prerecorded voices. While a voice line plays, background
volume is reduced temporarily; it returns after speech finishes.
OGG is used on compatible browsers; the supplied MP3 is the compatibility fallback.
Music, game, and teacher answer materials work offline when kept together.

All 39 scenes, 157 pre-recorded voice files, two-team rotation, 8-minute timer,
20-key target, and teacher answer codes are unchanged.

DEPLOY ONLINE:
Upload the contents of this ZIP as a single static site, keeping index.html and
assets/ together. Replace all prior version files and clear old browser caches.
Initial music load depends on network speed. No internet host can guarantee zero
latency on every connection. Voice prompts remain separate and pre-optimized.

The teacher folder contains answer keys. DO NOT PUBLISH it on the public student
site; keep that folder only on the teacher's device.
