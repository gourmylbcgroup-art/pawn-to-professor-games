WORLD FOOD TRAVEL — HTML5 TOUCHSCREEN GAME

How to host:
1. Upload the entire world-food-travel-main folder to your web host.
2. Keep the folder structure unchanged.
3. Open index.html in a modern browser.

Gameplay:
- 2–4 players choose Foodie tokens.
- 8 vocabulary cards shuffle once at the start and then stay fixed.
- The target always comes from the NEXT board square.
- Correct card: move +1 and the same player continues.
- Wrong card: DO NOT move backward. The token stays on its square.
- After a wrong answer, the correct hidden card is shown briefly as a learning cue.
- After some wrong answers, a Rescue Challenge appears (35% chance):
  * Two large picture choices appear.
  * Correct rescue answer: the same player keeps playing.
  * Wrong rescue answer: the next traveler plays.
- If no Rescue Challenge appears, the next traveler plays.
- First player to reach space 24 wins.

Fun bonus rules:
- Passport Bonus: random after a correct answer; +1 extra space.
- Jet Boost: after 3 correct answers in a row; +2 spaces.
- Food & Country Challenge: occasionally after a food answer; correct = +1 bonus space.
- Speed Challenge is not used.

Wrong-answer voice:
- “Oh no! Travel delay!”
- No “Don’t worry. Stay here!” line is used.
- Rescue voice: “Rescue challenge!”
- Rescue success: “Great! Keep traveling!”
- Rescue miss / normal end of wrong turn: “Good try! Next traveler!”

Sound:
- Voice prompts are prerecorded LOCAL MP3 files in assets/audio/voice/.
- No online/browser text-to-speech is used.
- Game sound effects are generated locally in the browser.
- No external JavaScript libraries or internet APIs are required for gameplay.
