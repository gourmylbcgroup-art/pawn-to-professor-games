How to Play

👀 Look at the next board square.
🧠 Remember where the 8 cards are.
👆 Touch one of the 8 face-down cards and find the matching picture.
✅ Correct = +1 space and keep playing.
🛂 Passport Bonus may appear after a correct answer = +1 extra space.
✈️ After 3 correct answers in a row, you get a Jet Boost = +2 spaces.
🍔 On some food cards, answer the Food & Country Challenge correctly for +1 bonus space.
❌ Wrong = stay on your square — no moving backward.
💡 After a wrong card, the correct card is shown briefly.
🛟 Sometimes a Rescue Challenge appears. Correct = keep playing; wrong = next traveler.
