FAIR CLASSROOM MODE

Balance changes:
- Maximum 3 correct card finds per turn.
- Maximum 1 movement bonus per turn (Passport, Food Bonus, or Jet Boost).
- Passport Bonus chance reduced from 28% to 20%.
- Food & Country Challenge chance reduced from 45% to 30%.
- Rescue Challenge chance reduced from 35% to 30%.
- Jet Boost changed from +2 spaces to +1 space.
- Three consecutive correct answers trigger Jet Boost only if no other movement bonus was already earned that turn.
- A Food Challenge and Passport Bonus cannot both trigger from the same correct answer.
- Existing rule preserved: a wrong answer does not reveal the correct card and does not move the player backward.
