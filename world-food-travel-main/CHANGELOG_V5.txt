WORLD FOOD TRAVEL V5 — POSITIVE WRONG-ANSWER RULE

Changed carefully from V4:
- Wrong answer no longer moves the player backward.
- Wrong answer voice is now: “Oh no! Travel delay!”
- “Don’t worry. Stay here!” is NOT used.
- Correct hidden card is shown briefly after a wrong answer.
- 35% chance of a Rescue Challenge after a wrong answer.
- Rescue Challenge shows two large picture choices.
- Correct rescue: “Great! Keep traveling!” and the same player continues.
- Wrong rescue: “Good try! Next traveler!” and the turn passes.
- If no rescue appears, “Good try! Next traveler!” and the turn passes.
- Speed Challenge remains removed.
- Existing Passport Bonus, Jet Boost and Food & Country Challenge remain.
- Passport Bonus verified/fixed to exactly +1 extra space.
- All voice audio is local; no online TTS.
