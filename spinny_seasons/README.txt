SPINNY SEASONS - FIXED AUDIO AND FLASHCARDS
Open index.html after extracting the entire ZIP, or upload the spinny_seasons folder to your website.
- Click the ten picture-only flashcards on the left to enlarge them.
- Mime Cards opens a separate in-page picture-only grid; click any card to enlarge and return to wheel.
- SPIN plays the wheel spinning sound only.
- START MIME plays the supplied Roblox music and begins the default 1:30 timer.
- PAUSE/LISTEN pauses music and countdown. TRY AGAIN restarts the same challenge and music/timer.
- Answers stay hidden until CORRECT.
- All media assets are packaged locally.
Audio/browser note: most browsers require a first tap/click before sound can play. The spinning audio is time-stretched to the wheel duration using Web Audio when supported. Audio latency may vary by device and browser.
