CORE RULES

Roll → Move → Draw → Speak

  1. Roll the die and move your puppet token.
  2. On a normal space, draw a flashcard and complete the English challenge.
  3. Correct: stay on the space. Need help: teacher supports; repeat, then the turn ends. Never move backwards.
  4. Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner: complete the meal challenge correctly → play again.
  5. REST: stomach is full → miss 1 turn.
  6. Night Market: say the Night Market sentence → miss 1 turn because it is late.
  7. Every 3 correct answers in a row earns a Yunlin Star Boost (+1 space).
  8. First puppet to FINISH wins. Exact roll is not required.
