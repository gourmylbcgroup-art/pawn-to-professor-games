NUMBER ROPE BATTLE — SPEED MIX

Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.

Rules:
- Blue and Red have independent quizzes and can answer at the same time.
- Numbers are 0–10.
- Questions 1–10 on each team side are digit -> word.
- From question 11 onward on that side, questions randomly alternate between digit -> word and word -> digit.
- Every question has 3 randomized choices.
- Correct answer: that team pulls the rope one step.
- Wrong answer: no rope movement; that side gets a new question.
- First team to pull the rope 5 steps from center wins.

Use the fullscreen button for classroom play.
