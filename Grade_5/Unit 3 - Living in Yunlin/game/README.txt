WHO AM I? scaffolding
1. The family flashcard is shown with ONLY the printed answer label covered.
2. Students choose the family member from 3 choices.
3. Correct choice reveals the complete original flashcard.
4. The game models: He/She is my ______.
5. The linked job/action/time/place cards appear.
6. The question plays by voice.
7. Student says the full answer. Teacher taps Correct or Try Again.
8. WHO AM I? challenges are worth 2 stars.

The game still uses full-card display (object-fit: contain) for normal and revealed cards.
Questions and model answers use the browser/device English speech engine so no remote MP3 streaming is required.
